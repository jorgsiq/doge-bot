## 1. What Đoge Bot is

Đoge Bot is a free bot for Discord servers. Among other things, it plays music in voice channels, creates temporary voice rooms, posts alerts about free games and deals, posts a photo of a shiba every day, welcomes new members, logs nickname and avatar changes, cleans channels and offers interaction commands such as `/pet`, `/kiss`, `/slap` and `/ship`.

Each feature is switched on or off by the admins of each server, with the `/setup` command.

## 2. Who can use it

To use the bot, you need a Discord account and must follow Discord's [Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms) and [Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines), including the minimum age they require.

Whoever adds and sets up the bot in a server is responsible for how it is used there. Some features delete messages permanently, such as `/delete`, the daily cleanup and the commands channel, and the bot keeps no copy of what was deleted. Admins also choose which channels get announcements, logs and alerts, and who can use each command, in **Server Settings → Integrations**.

## 5. Third-party content and services

To work, the bot fetches content from third-party services, such as YouTube Music and Audius (music), Spotify (the names of songs in playlists and albums), nekos.best and otakugifs (GIFs), CheapShark, the Epic Games Store and Steam (games and prices) and Wikimedia Commons (shiba photos). That content belongs to its authors and follows each service's rules. Đoge Bot is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them, and has no control over what they offer or stop offering.

Music is streamed live to the voice channel: the bot does not store or distribute audio files. Whoever requests a song is responsible for what they request.

## 6. Data

The bot keeps only what it needs to work: each server's settings (the features switched on and the IDs of the chosen channels and roles), the open temporary rooms and who owns each one, and which alerts and photos were already posted, so they aren't repeated. It does not read or store the content of messages.

With welcomes and logs switched on, the bot posts the name, avatar and ID of whoever joins, leaves or changes their nickname or avatar to the channel the server chose. It may also send direct messages: a welcome to whoever joins a server with that feature on, and invites to temporary rooms.

The details are in the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md). To ask for a server's data to be removed, get in touch through the email at the end of this page.
